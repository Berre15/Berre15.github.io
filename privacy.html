Privacy policy

NOVA Cinematic Studio ("the app") is a personal tool used by its owner, Bert Pauwels, to upload videos to his own YouTube channel.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, manage the owner's own uploads (title, description, privacy setting, playlists) and read the statistics of the owner's videos (views, likes, comments).

What is stored

The OAuth token and the video statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared or sent to any other party. No data about viewers or other YouTube users is collected or stored.

Third parties

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored statistics are deleted on request; the app keeps data only as long as it is needed for the owner's own channel reports.

Contact

Questions: via the contact e-mail shown on the Google consent screen of this app.
